# Sample traffic video

`cars-night-960.mp4` is a 960 × 540, silent H.264 adaptation of [Cars Passing by at Night](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cars_Passing_by_at_Night.webm) by Maximilian Schönherr. The original was resized and transcoded; the scene was not otherwise edited. Both the original and this adaptation are shared under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). It is a short sample that the demo replays. Its counts are illustrative, not traffic accuracy results.
